Be the Change. Save a Life. Become an Organ Donation Ambassador.

You Have More Power Than You Think

Imagine this: someone your age is lying in a hospital bed, waiting for a kidney, a liver, a heart. Their entire future — their dreams, their relationships, their laughter — depends on one person saying yes to organ donation.

That person could be inspired by you.

MOHAN Foundation's Organ Donation Ambassador Training Program is looking for people who are ready to do something that truly matters. Not just share a post. Not just wear a ribbon. But actually go out there, have real conversations, and move people to act.

No medical degree required. Just passion, curiosity, and the willingness to show up.

Here's the Reality — and Why It Needs YOU

India has made progress. In 2023, over 1,000 deceased donors helped save 2,935 lives through transplants. That's incredible — and it happened because people chose to speak up. But the gap is still massive:

3,00,000+ Patients Awaiting List
16,000 Total Donors
< 1 Per Million Deceased Donor Rate

The biggest barrier? It's not biology. It's fear, myths, and silence — all things a conﬁdent, well-informed young person can change. That's not a small thing. That's everything.

So What Does an Ambassador Actually Do?

Forget the corporate-sounding title. Here's what being an ambassador really looks like:

You talk to people — friends, family, classmates, your college canteen crowd — about why organ donation matters and how easy it is to pledge

You bust myths — "Will doctors not try to save me if I'm a donor?" (No, they absolutely will.) You'll know exactly how to answer questions like this

You create buzz — social media posts, campus events, webinars, info desks — you bring organ donation into spaces where it's never been discussed

You earn recognition — a nationally recognised certiﬁcate, a spotlight in our newsletter, and the knowledge that your work directly saved lives

The Training: Built for Busy Young People

We get it — you have classes, internships, side projects, and a social life. Our program is designed to ﬁt into your schedule, not the other way around.

Learn at your own pace | 4 hours | 100% Online

Think of this as your crash course in organ donation — covering everything from the science and legality to the ethics, religious perspectives, and the deeply human stories behind transplantation.

Fee: Rs. 500/- (Completely FREE if you're from a donor family)

Finish the course and earn your oﬃcial online certiﬁcate. Add it to your resume. Wear it with pride.

2 hours | Interactive | Online

This isn't a lecture — it's a conversation. You'll get to hear directly from:

  • Organ recipients who got a second chance at life
  • Donor families who turned grief into the greatest gift
  • Experts, clinicians and NGO advocates who've been in the ﬁeld for years

These sessions will give you the conﬁdence, the language, and the heart to go out and do this work.

1 Initiative | Action-Oriented | Field Or Online

Once you've completed the ﬁrst two modules, it's your turn to take the lead. Design and run one awareness initiative — it could be:

  • A stall at your college fest
  • An Instagram or YouTube campaign
  • A talk at your school or workplace
  • A webinar, a survey, or a community drive

See the impact of your campaign. Complete one initiative and you earn your Organ Donation Ambassador Certiﬁcate.

You Won't Be Doing This Alone

When you join, you become part of a growing tribe of young change-makers across India who share your values and your drive. Connect, collaborate, and celebrate wins together in our exclusive Ambassador community:

Join the Ambassador Facebook Group

This Is More Than a Certiﬁcate

Yes, you'll gain a credential. Yes, you'll build real-world communication and advocacy skills.

But more than that — you will have saved lives. Every conversation you start, every pledge you inspire, every myth you put to rest — it adds up to someone getting a transplant, a family keeping their person, a future that almost didn't happen. You don't need to be a doctor to do that. You just need to start.

Ready to Jump In?

Join the movement. Earn your certiﬁcate. Change lives.
