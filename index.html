Become an Organ Donation Ambassador

Are you ready to make a lasting impact and save lives? Join MOHAN Foundation's transformative Organ Donation Ambassador Training Program and be the voice that sparks hope and change. This program is carefully crafted to empower you with the right tools, knowledge, and confidence to champion organ donation. A well-trained ambassador has the power to shift public perception, drive meaningful change, and create a ripple effect of awareness in the cause of organ donation.

The Challenge We Face Today

India is at a critical juncture when it comes to organ donation. In 2023 Indian marked a significant milestone by having more than 1000 deceased donors leading to 2,935 life saving transplants through these donors.

Presently, there are over 3,00,000 patients awaiting organ donations in India with only 16,000 donors of which 80% are living donors. This shortfall in organ transplants leads to 20 deaths daily. For the past ten years, the rate of deceased organ donation in India has consistently remained below one donor per million individuals. (Jain and Bhalla 2023 site).

Adding to this shortfall, the low rate of donations is fuelled by misinformation, cultural misconceptions, and stigma.

How You will Make a Difference

Change begins with individuals like you - compassionate change-makers with the courage to shift perspectives and challenge the narrative.

As an ambassador, your actions will directly impact the lives of patients suffering from organ failure and provide hope to their families.

Through our comprehensive training, you’ll gain the skills to:

  • Communicate the value of organ donation effectively and compassionately.
  • Address questions, dispel myths, and help people overcome hesitation.
  • Encourage communities to pledge their support and save lives.

Be a part of the Organ Donation Ambassadors movement - a cause that has the power to save lives.

About the Training
Forget the corporate-sounding title. Here's what being an ambassador really looks like:

We get it — you have classes, internships, side projects, and a social life. Our program is designed to ﬁt into your schedule, not the other way around.

  • MODULE 1:     ►
    4-hour, learn-at-your-pace
    "Gift of Life" Certificate Course on Organ Donation

    This course covers key aspects of organ donation and transplantation, including legal and ethical considerations, religious views, and the importance of donation.

    For a small fee of Rs. 500/-, you will gain valuable knowledge and receive an online certificate upon completion. However if you are an organ donor or from a family of an organ donor we offer the course FREE to you.
  • MODULE 2:     ►
    2-hour Interactive Sessions on Zoom:

    Engage in activities that foster empathy and a deeper understanding of the cause. You will have the opportunity to interact with organ recipients, donor families, and experts in the field to enrich your perspective further and give you the confidence to take up the cause.
  • MODULE 3:     ►
    Organ Donation Awareness and Fundraising Activities
    It’s time to take action!

    After completing the first two modules, you will be ready to organize awareness programs such as information desks, talks, webinars, surveys, and social media campaigns. Upon completing one awareness initiative, you will earn the coveted Organ Donation Ambassador certificate.

    As a bonus, you will have the chance to be featured in our e-newsletter and, if you engage in innovative initiatives, you may be invited to our exclusive webinars.

    Our Ambassadors have an exclusive Facebook page too - https://www.facebook.com/groups/959271397549505

    Why This Matters:
    Your participation as an Organ Donation Ambassador could be the difference between life and death for those in need of transplants. By raising awareness and encouraging pledges, you are directly helping save lives and giving hope to countless families.
